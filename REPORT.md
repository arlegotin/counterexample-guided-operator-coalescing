## Interpretation and scope

The original study remains primary. The fixed fresh-component repeat is exploratory; the pooled analysis and the eighteen-pair restrictions are secondary and never replace it. All scoring rules see the same fitted states. Their equal-pool scoring comparison is not a complete efficient random-only acquisition pipeline. Regret is relative to the best sampled candidate, not the full pair population. Point estimates average source-component token means; the Parquet preserves genuine raw KL/NLL sums and target denominators for independent reduction. Bootstrap draws resample whole source components and recompute candidate selection and the sampled oracle; tokens and overlapping pairs are not independent samples.

The canonical global learning curve, the local conditional teacher-suffix loss and the preliminary dense quantization frontier measure different objects. In particular, the global curve uses eight development components while the dense frontier uses thirty-two. Their KL values cannot be directly compared as matched superiority. The local operation is conditional replacement-through-teacher-suffix KL, not whole-model post-local KL. The original intervention-damage endpoint likewise remains conditional and separate from natural damage; the fresh repeat collected no new intervention endpoint.

The untrained format anchor defines bytes only. Dense quantization is the preliminary deployment hurdle, with oversized points retained. The study stops before a trained native shared model at the ceiling, protected capability confirmation, runtime comparison, full SV or compiler evaluation. The scope and unavailable stages are listed below.

The teacher's development competence screen is retained in [its receipt](provenance/teacher-qualification-2026-10-27.json) and [the scope record](PROGRESS.md). Weak arithmetic/composition screens cannot establish reasoning preservation. Model, method seed, source-component counts, fitting exposure, numerical backend and costs limit every claim. Exact replay reproduces retained candidate states; it adds physical spending and no unique learning exposure. Teacher/student dispatch counts include chunked head work and are not counts of complete-model evaluations. Unique-token and FLOP counters remain unavailable despite legacy zero fields.

Blueprint §11 audit: scientific attribution is unresolved and prior-art overlap prevents an invention claim; experimental validity is limited by finite candidates, one-step optimization, near-best sensitivity and the absence of protected confirmation; implementation feasibility is demonstrated for the recorded local primitives, bundles and preliminary Q formats only. No compiler/joint trained-composition or end-to-end deployment comparison ran. The source comparisons in the [original critical review](docs/analysis/2026-10-27-blueprint-review.md) must be refreshed before external publication. This local report does not publish externally or expand rights to restricted data or third-party code.
